# Unjung oblique window review

The elevated side camera made the recessed front glazing almost disappear. Two source trials moved the complete windows 0.18 m outward and replaced their opaque backing boxes with open perimeter frames. Actual Phase23/24 rendering did not establish that either trial solved the symptom.

Astra subsequently checked rays from the actual side camera in local coordinates `(55.667, 52.690, 66.341)`. At the trial pane front plane `z = 23.775`, the ray from a lower window center at `y = 3` reaches the solid parapet at `y = 4.570`. A ray from an upper window center at `y = 6.4` reaches the roof slab at approximately `y = 7.863`, with additional fascia occlusion. The reported sampling found approximately 93% of lower and 90% of upper glazing structurally occluded at this elevated pose.

This evidence disconfirmed the proposed frame/body depth conflict as the explanation. Both Unjung window trials were reverted to the original generic window positions and backing boxes. The source-supported solid cream parapet remains intact. A lower front/side camera pitch of approximately −12° to −15° is the appropriate next visual check; the roof and parapet should not be removed simply to make windows visible from above.

These geometry dimensions remain modelling estimates. This note records the investigation and does not claim photo-identical construction or completed lower-camera acceptance.
