Graduate **axis clarification:** the front photograph supports the canopy opening, doorway and central stair route belonging to one aligned entrance composition. It does not establish the exact central axis of the entire irregular building. The current code's 4.65m difference between canopy and door centers is a diagnostic of inconsistent modeled coordinates, **not** evidence to move a real canopy exactly 4.65m. Rectify/compare the front image and define the assembly's relative axes before selecting estimated model coordinates. Round profiles and visible support count can be corrected from existing evidence without waiting for every absolute dimension.

For every new photograph, retain the original file and EXIF, capture date, an annotated camera-position sketch, and at least one scale reference for the relevant plane. Record whether vegetation hides the edge being inferred. Measurements may be approximate if their uncertainty is explicit; do not label a photo-proportioned value as surveyed.
