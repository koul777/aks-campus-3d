## Runtime and usability limits

`render/runtime.json` reports 11,965 ms readiness, Intel Arc/ANGLE rendering, empty errors/warnings/failures, and 19 settled view records. Its checks include model/label controls, pointer navigation, map dialog, direct building picking and responsive layouts. `render/idle.json` reports 19 ready/visible models and zero new rendered frames during the idle sample. I inspected these existing records but did not independently rerun their assertions.

The screenshots corroborate model loading and a functioning map display. They do not establish that every entry is navigable or correctly framed. In particular, `targetDirectionDot = 1` for Boin does not prevent substantial clipping or reveal its approach. A center-directed camera is insufficient visual coverage evidence.

At 375 px the facility list is readable and controls remain available, but the overview's clustered labels overlap heavily. In several desktop selections a roof-height label projects over a different building or bare ground; without leader lines, these are ambiguous. The full app's sidebar provides useful photographs and explicit uncertainty notices, which are appropriate. Those notices do not erase visible facade contradictions.

No independent build was run. No exact height, footprint, year-current terrain, material reflectance, back-face identity, inaccessible interior or full accessibility route was verified. Older satellite and panorama views do not establish the completed 2024 dorm footprint. The source set mixes view dates and camera directions; I did not treat all differences of season, illumination or vegetation as geometry errors.

## At most two small high-value improvements

1. **Reframe Boin's selected destination.** Pull back and choose a three-quarter heading that includes both principal blocks, the low glazed connector and the near ground approach. Use the two preserved 2024 blog photographs as view-direction evidence, not as metric dimensions. Validate the entire projected building bounds with margins in an actual new screenshot, including the 375 px layout. This is a small camera change; it should not require new geometry or surveying.
2. **Make the Gugeun and Jinhyeon photographed bay windows visible on the shown entry faces.** First check whether the existing projected-window details are assigned to the opposite side or are missing from the compiled model; the snapshot's `data/building-model.ts` already contains a `photoBayWindows` detail routine. Use `...facility-review__gugeun-detail.jpg` and `...facility-review__jinhyeon.jpg` to preserve Gugeun's projecting upper versus flatter lower openings and Jinhyeon's projecting openings on both levels. Restrict any quick correction to these clearly photographed faces and compare a new render from the corresponding entrance side. Do not invent unseen elevations or claim exact dimensions.

Neither proposed small change alone would yield real-identical fidelity. Terrain integration, pond banks, stairs/entrances, material hierarchy and source-faithful facade depth remain larger work.
