# Haku Phase25 internal design review

2026-10-10. Reviewed actual product, close, corner and named underside screenshots in `artifacts/haku-curved-review-phase25`, against the Phase21 close/corner renders, official project hak02/03/05/06 and the small official image `references/hakuijeong.jpg`.

**Adopt as a bounded visual improvement**, subject to root's geometry-clearance audit and motion inspection. This is an internal design judgment by the author of the shape contract; it is not an independent sealed review or formal quality score.

The continuous roof sweep replaces the old straight pyramid with separately angular corner lip. Curved hips now follow the surface, and the ceramic end row is visibly separate above the timber rafter ends. The four screenshots show no detached roof bands or obvious timber punctures. Existing platform, pale piers, bridge and access gap remain visually consistent with the prior model.

The apex still reads tall and steep; the concavity is more emphatic than in some source perspectives. Do not exaggerate it further or call this a measured fit. The grey roof remains darker than sunlit source photographs, ceramic discs are schematic, timber lacks the real surface variation, and the signboard/carving details remain absent or simplified. These limitations do not erase the specific silhouette improvement.

The file named `hakuijeong-underside.png` is a lower frontal view but does not reveal the complete roof underside or joinery. It therefore cannot establish full underside fidelity. Root's actual-triangle clearance audit can address geometric penetration separately; the upcoming orbit should check flicker, cracks, floating cap pieces and transitions between views. No claim of reference-photo quality or full faithful reconstruction is made.
